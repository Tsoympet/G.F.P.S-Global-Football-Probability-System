# Limitations & Transparency

- Simulation only: no execution, payments, or wagering flows.
- Odds snapshots may be stale; when missing, backtests fall back to fair odds.
- Settlement depends on ingested results; pending fixtures surface data-quality warnings.
- Current release focuses on 1X2 markets; other markets are treated as “other” and may not settle automatically.
- Correlation is approximated by fixture-level deduping; true statistical correlation requires deeper modelling.
- Small sample sizes are flagged; treat ROI and drawdown outputs cautiously.
- No guarantee of profit. GFPS provides probabilistic analytics only.
