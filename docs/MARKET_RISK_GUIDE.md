# Market Risk Commentary Guide

The Bookmaker View surfaces market-risk notes as a trading desk would.

## Reading the sections
- **A. Market Read** – Snapshot of fair vs book price and any shading hints.
- **B. Risk Assessment** – Edge/EV signal, CLV proxy (if present), and whether the market is live or pre-match.
- **C. Trap Indicators** – Labels:
  - **Low**: near-fair pricing, little shading observed.
  - **Medium**: price above fair or thin confidence where books lean on margin.
  - **High**: generous price on low-probability sides or narrative/public pull.
- **D. Timing Advice** – Notes on stale numbers, missing tape, or expected steam windows.
- **E. Confidence Warning** – Reminders about model error bounds and public/news shocks.

## Usage principles
- Keep the panel off until you want a desk-style second opinion.
- Treat indicators as risk guards, not triggers.
- Cross-check with live odds tape when available; static inputs can mask late steam.

## Limitations disclosed
- Missing CLV or odds snapshots reduce certainty on movement.
- Kickoff time gaps limit timing commentary accuracy.
- The module does not encourage wagers or promise outcomes; it highlights where the model might be wrong.
